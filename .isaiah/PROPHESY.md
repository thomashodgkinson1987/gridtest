# Prophesy

## Guiding Statement

Project Chimera shall be a procedurally generated, science-fantasy roguelike designed to serve as a framework for deep, systemic simulation and emergent, player-driven narrative.

## Foundational Principles

1.  **Systemic World:** The core of the project is a world that operates on persistent, interconnected systems. Factions, ecosystems, and historical events shall be simulated independently of the player's presence, creating a world that is not a stage, but a living machine.
2.  **Procedural Generation:** All elements, from stellar bodies down to the properties of an individual item, shall be generated algorithmically. The goal is not simply variety, but the creation of a universe with coherent, discoverable rules that ensure no two playthroughs are alike.
3.  **Emergent Narrative:** The project will actively avoid authored, linear plotlines. Narrative shall emerge from the interplay of the world's systems and the player's choices. The primary metric of success will be the quality and variety of anecdotes generated by play.
4.  **Transformative Progression:** Character development shall focus on acquiring new capabilities that fundamentally alter gameplay, rather than simple statistical increases. Mutation, cybernetics, and psionics should open new verbs to the player, not just modify existing ones.
5.  **Scale Fidelity:** The architecture must support seamless transitions between vastly different scales of interaction: from the turn-based, tile-level actions on a planet's surface to the macro-level, turn-based navigation of a starship within a solar system.

## Precedents & Influences

-   ***Caves of Qud:*** Serves as the primary model for systemic world-building and emergent narrative. We will study its approach to lore, factions, history generation, and its unique descriptive language.
-   ***Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead:*** Provides the benchmark for player agency, survival mechanics, and crafting depth. Its philosophy of "if you can think it, you can do it" is a key driver for our interaction design.
-   ***No Man's Sky:*** Establishes the technical and aesthetic target for seamless scale transition. Its visual identity and the feeling of boundless exploration are the primary sources of inspiration for the project's presentation.
